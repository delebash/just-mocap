Gaze ruler

Any camera works: an iPhone (Live Link Face or the camera app), an Android phone, or a webcam through OBS. Put it beside or just under the screen, facing you, in good light. Sit about 60 cm from the screen.

  1. Start recording on the camera.
  2. Press Start. The screen flashes white three times, a dot visits nine spots twice, then it flashes three times again. About a minute.
  3. Keep your head still and follow the dot with your eyes only.
  4. Stop recording after the last flashes, and put the video in E:\Dev\Web\just-mocap\in.

Optional, for angles: your distance to the screen and the screen's visible width.
